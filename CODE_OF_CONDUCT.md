# Code of conduct

This project is a place for constructive, respectful collaboration. Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space, is expected to:

- be considerate and welcoming, whatever someone's background, identity or level of experience;
- discuss ideas and code, not people, and give and take criticism graciously;
- assume good intent, and ask before concluding otherwise;
- respect others' privacy: do not post other people's personal information, private package names or internal details without their permission.

Harassment, insults, sexualised language or imagery, threats, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that break these expectations and may restrict or ban anyone who does so repeatedly or seriously.

To report a concern, email [conduct@semverity.dev](mailto:conduct@semverity.dev). Reports are handled confidentially by the maintainers.
